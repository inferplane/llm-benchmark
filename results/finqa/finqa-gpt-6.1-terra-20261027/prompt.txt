const_1, const_m1 (-1), const_0, and other numeric const_ values.

- usd, usd_thousand, usd_million, usd_billion: dollar amounts in the declared scale.
- count, count_thousand: counts in the declared scale.
- boolean: only for the "yes" or "no" result of greater.
Use these exact unit names. Identify the unit requested by the question; when no scale
is requested, preserve the scale explicitly stated in the document. For yearly average

Always declare the unit; never change it just to match an input table heading.

declared unit. The evaluator never guesses a missing unit or repairs a wrong calculation.
